Privacy policy – Invio agenzie

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Invio agenzie is a private tool operated by Wajdi Ben Slama for his own use. It has a single user: its owner.

Google data accessed

  • Gmail: sends emails from the owner's account and reads recent incoming messages only to detect replies and bounces to those emails.
  • Google Drive: reads and updates files in one folder owned by the owner (the contact list, email templates, CV and log).

How the data is used

The data is used only to run this tool for its owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Data stays in the owner's Gmail and Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contacts

Businesses contacted by the tool can ask not to be contacted again by replying "no thanks" to any email; they are then excluded permanently. Contact: wajdibenslama.com.
